Privacy Policy

Little Explorers Quest is a learning game for young children (phonics, maths, reading, general knowledge, geography and science). Last updated: 27 November 2026.

In short: the game has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your child's progress stays on your own device unless a grown-up chooses to turn on cloud sync.

What the app stores on your device

  • A player name (a first name or nickname chosen by the grown-up), the avatar the child builds, points, levels finished, which words and sounds were easy or tricky, and a short log of days played.
  • This is kept in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent anywhere unless you use cloud sync or save a backup file yourself.
  • A grown-up PIN, stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value.

Cloud sync (optional, grown-ups only)

  • Cloud sync is off unless a grown-up signs in inside the Grown-up corner. It is never needed to play.
  • If used, we store the grown-up's email address and the players' progress (the same data as above) with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Only the signed-in account can read or write its own data.
  • No data is collected from children directly, and no child's real name, photo, address or location is requested.
  • Deleting your data: in the Grown-up corner you can delete a player, and choose "Delete my cloud account" to remove your online account and everything saved with it.

Microphone (optional)

  • The microphone is only used when a child taps a button to say a sound or read aloud, so the game can check what she said. It is optional and can be switched off.
  • The app does not record, keep or upload audio. The speech is turned into text by your device's or browser's own speech service (for example Apple or Google), which has its own privacy policy. The app only keeps a "right or tricky" result.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party content.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Links that leave the game are behind a grown-up check.

Children

The game is designed for children aged about 5 to 8, to be used with or set up by a parent or carer. Grown-ups control the PIN, backups and cloud sync.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions or requests to delete data: please open an issue on the project page, github.com/yuvedha/phonics-quest/issues.
